Direct movement

Obliges the driver to drive only straight. The sign applies to the intersection of carriageways in front of which the appropriate sign is installed; the sign does not apply to minibuses. The sign, which is installed at the beginning of a road section, applies to the nearest intersection. The sign does not prohibit turning right into a yard or entering other adjacent territory.

Right-hand traffic

Obliges the driver to drive only on the right. The sign applies to the intersection of carriageways in front of which the appropriate sign is installed; the sign does not apply to minibuses.

Left-hand traffic

Obliges the driver to move only to the left. The sign also allows for turning. The sign applies to the intersection of carriageways in front of which the appropriate sign is installed; the sign does not apply to minibuses.

Moving straight or to the right

Obliges the driver to drive only straight or to the right. The sign applies to the intersection of carriageways in front of which the appropriate sign is installed; the sign does not apply to minibuses.

Moving straight or left

Obliges the driver to drive only straight or left. The sign also allows for turning. The sign applies to the intersection of carriageways before which the appropriate sign is installed; the sign does not apply to minibuses.

Movement to the right or left

Obliges the driver to move only to the right or left. The sign also allows for turning. The sign applies to the intersection of carriageways in front of which the appropriate sign is installed; the sign does not apply to minibuses.

Going around an obstacle from the right

Indicates the direction of passing a dividing strip, safety island, or any obstacle only from the right.

Going around an obstacle from the left

Indicates the direction of passing a dividing strip, safety island, or any obstacle only from the left.

Going around an obstacle from the right or left

Indicates the direction of going around a dividing strip, safety island, or any obstacle from the right or left.

Circular motion

Allows movement in the directions indicated by the arrows at an intersection where circular traffic is organized.

Bicycle path

A path intended for bicycle traffic, which is structurally separated from another road or other road elements. The 4.4 sign is placed as the main sign at the beginning of the bicycle path, and repeatedly after each intersection with the road.

Pedestrian path

A path intended for pedestrian traffic, separated from the road. The 4.5 sign is placed as the main sign at the beginning of the pedestrian path, and repeatedly after each intersection with the road.

Minimum speed limit

Sets the minimum speed limit for movement on the road. The sign is valid after the sign, from the place where the sign is installed to the nearest intersection, and in the absence of an intersection in a settlement, to the end of the settlement.

End of minimum speed limit zone

End of the sign's area of ​​effect.

Direction of movement of vehicles carrying dangerous goods

Indicates the direction of movement permitted by a vehicle carrying dangerous goods, which, according to Georgian legislation, must be marked with a dangerous goods sign.

Anti-skid chains are required

A section of road on which a vehicle must have anti-skid chains attached to at least two of its drive wheels.
