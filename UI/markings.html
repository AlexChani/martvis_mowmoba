Marking 1.1.

Separates traffic flows moving in opposite directions and marks the boundaries of traffic lanes on dangerous sections; marks the boundaries of the carriageway to which entry is prohibited; marks the boundaries of the place where vehicles can stand and the edges of the carriageway that do not belong to the motorway under traffic conditions. Crossing line 1.1 is prohibited. It is permitted to cross line 1.1, which marks the edge of the carriageway and is located on the driver's right side, to stop vehicles on the edge of the road.

Marking 1.2.

The solid wide line marks the edges of the carriageway on a motorway. 1.2 Crossing the line is permitted to stop a vehicle on the edge of the road.

Marking 1.3.

Separates traffic flows moving in opposite directions on a road with four or more lanes. 1.3 Crossing the line is prohibited.

Marking 1.4.

Indicates a place where parking is prohibited; used independently or in conjunction with sign 3.27; drawn on the edge of the carriageway or on the top of the curb.

Marking 1.5.

Separates traffic flows moving in opposite directions on a road with two or three lanes of traffic; marks the boundaries of traffic lanes when there are two or more lanes intended for traffic in one direction. 1.5 Crossing the line is permitted from any side.

Marking 1.6.

Approach line – (a dashed line with a line three times the length of the lines between them) warns of approaching markings 1.1 or 1.11 separating traffic flows moving in opposite or converging directions. Crossing the 1.6 line is permitted from any side.

Marking 1.7.

A dashed line with short strokes and equal spacing marks the traffic lanes within an intersection. 1.7 Crossing the line is permitted from any side.

Marking 1.8.

Wide dashed line - marks the boundaries between the acceleration or braking lanes and the main lane of the carriageway (at an intersection, at a level crossing, in a bus stop zone, etc.). 1.8 Crossing the line is permitted from any side.

Marking 1.9.

Marks the boundaries of reverse traffic lanes, separates traffic flows moving in opposite directions (when reverse traffic lights are off) on roads that are reverse-oriented.

Marking 1.10.

Indicates a place where standing is prohibited; used independently or in conjunction with sign 3.28; drawn at the edge of the carriageway or above the curb.

Marking 1.11.

Separates traffic flows moving in opposite or same direction on a section of the road where regrouping is allowed only from one lane; marks the place of turning, exit and entrance from a parking lot, etc., where movement is allowed only on one side. 1.11 Crossing the line is allowed from the side of a broken line, as well as from the side of a continuous line, but only when overtaking or passing.

Marking 1.12.

The "stop line" indicates the place where the driver must stop when there is a 2.5 sign, or at a traffic light or traffic controller's prohibitory signal.

Marking 1.13.

Indicates the place where the driver must stop and give way to a vehicle moving on the crossing road, if necessary.

Marking 1.14.1.

"Zebra" refers to an unregulated pedestrian crossing;

Marking 1.14.2.

"Zebra" denotes an unregulated pedestrian crossing; 1.14.2 The marking arrow indicates the direction of pedestrian movement.

Marking 1.14.3.

Indicates a pedestrian crossing where traffic is regulated by traffic lights.

Marking 1.15.

Marks the place where the bike path crosses the roadway.

Marking 1.16.1-1.16.3.

Indicates guide islands at the point of division or confluence of traffic flows.

Marking 1.17.

Indicates a bus stop and taxi stand.

Marking 1.18.

Indicates the permitted directions of traffic on the lanes of the intersection; used independently or in combination with signs 5.8.1, 5.8.2; marking with the image of a dead end indicates that turning onto the nearest carriageway is prohibited; marking permitting a left turn from the extreme left lane also permits turning from this lane.

Marking 1.19.

Warns of approaching a narrowing of the carriageway (a section where the number of lanes in a given direction is reduced) or a dividing line between traffic flows moving in the opposite direction, marked 1.1 or 1.11. In the first case, the marking 1.19 may be used in conjunction with signs 1.18.1–1.18.3.

Marking 1.20.

Warns about approaching mark 1.13.

Marking 1.21.

The inscription "სდექ" warns of approaching the 1.12 mark if it is used in conjunction with the 2.5 mark.

Marking 1.22.

Shows the road (route) number.

Marking 1.23.

Indicates a special lane intended for the movement of minibuses.
