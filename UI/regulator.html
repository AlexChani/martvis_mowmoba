Regulator Signals

Signal 1:

Both hands aside (or lowered)

What is happening on different sides of the regulator at this time:

Coming from the back:

All cars stop (pedestrians cross parallel to the back and chest)

Coming from the chest:

All cars stop (pedestrians cross parallel to the back and chest)

Coming from the sides (hands side):

All cars go only straight and to the right,

trams only go straight.

Signal 2:

Right hand forward

What is happening on different sides of the regulator at this time:

Coming from the right hand side:

All cars stop.

Coming from the back:

All cars stop (pedestrians cross parallel to the back)

Coming from the chest:

All cars drive only on the right.

Coming from the left (lowered) hand side:

The tram only goes to the left,

all other cars - in any direction

Signal 3:

Right hand up

What is happening on different sides of the regulator at this time:

Coming from all four sides:

All cars stop,

all pedestrians stop,

patrol cars, fire trucks, and ambulances also stop.
