Motorway

A motorway on which the requirements of the motorway traffic regulations apply. The sign is placed at the beginning of the motorway or near it with the sign 8.1.1.

End of the motorway

Indicates the end of the road marked with sign 5.1. Sign 5.2 is erected at the end of the motorway, at the exit from the motorway and at distances of 400 and 1000 meters before the end of the motorway with sign 8.1.1.

Road

A road intended only for automobile and motorcycle traffic.

End of the road

Indicates the end of the road marked with sign 5.3.

One-way road

A road or carriageway on which vehicles travel in one direction over its entire width.

End of one-way road

Indicates the end of a road or carriageway marked with sign 5.5.

Driving on a one-way road

Indicates the exit onto a one-way road or carriageway. Signs shall be placed in front of all side entrances. Signs may not be placed at the intersection with the exit road from the adjacent territory if the territory can only be entered from the road marked with sign 5.5.

Driving on a one-way road

Indicates the exit onto a one-way road or carriageway. Signs shall be placed in front of all side entrances. Signs may not be placed at the intersection with the exit road from the adjacent territory if the territory can only be entered from the road marked with sign 5.5.

Reverse movement

The beginning of a section of road where the direction of traffic on one or more lanes can be reversed.

End of reverse movement

Indicates the end of the road section marked with sign 5.8.

Driving on a road with reverse traffic

Indicates the exit to the section of the road marked with sign 5.8. The sign is placed in front of all side exits. The sign may not be placed at the intersection with the exit road from the adjacent territory if the entry to this territory is possible only from the road marked with sign 5.8.

Road with a lane intended for minibuses

A road on which a minibus vehicle travels in a specially designated lane in the opposite direction to the general flow of vehicles. The sign applies to the traffic lane on which it is located.

End of a road with a lane intended for minibuses

Indicates the end of the road marked with sign 5.11.

Driving on a road with a lane intended for minibuses

Indicates the exit to the road marked with sign 5.11. The sign is placed in front of all side entrances. Signs may not be placed at the intersection with the exit road from the adjacent territory if the entry to this territory is possible only from the road marked with sign 5.11.

Driving on a road with a lane intended for minibuses

Indicates the exit to the road marked with sign 5.11. The sign is placed in front of all side entrances. Signs may not be placed at the intersection with the exit road from the adjacent territory if the entry to this territory is possible only from the road marked with sign 5.11.

Traffic lane intended for minibuses

A road on which a minibus vehicle moves in a specially designated lane in the same direction as the general flow of vehicles. The effect of the sign extends to the traffic lane on which it is located. The effect of a sign installed on the right side of the road extends to the far right lane of the carriageway.

Directions of traffic on lanes

Indicates the number of lanes and the permitted directions of movement on the lanes. Signs that allow turning from the extreme left lane also allow turning from the same lane. The effect of signs installed in front of an intersection applies to the entire intersection, unless other signs 5.15.1 and 5.15.2 installed on it indicate otherwise; the effect of signs does not apply to minibuses.

Directions of traffic on the lane

Indicates the permitted directions of movement on a lane. Signs that allow turning from the far left lane also allow turning from the same lane. The effect of signs installed in front of an intersection applies to the entire intersection, unless other signs 5.15.1 and 5.15.2 installed on it indicate otherwise; the effect of signs does not apply to minibuses.

Beginning of the lane

Indicates the beginning of a braking or additional lane at an uphill or branching and intersection. If sign 5.15.3 placed in front of an additional lane displays sign 4.6 (minimum speed limit), the driver of a vehicle that cannot continue to move at the indicated or higher speed on the main lane shall move to the additional lane.

Beginning of the lane

Indicates the beginning of the middle lane section on a three-lane road, which is intended for traffic in a given direction.

End of the lane

Indicates the end of a braking or additional lane on an uphill slope.

End of the lane

Indicates the end of the section of the lane on a three-lane road that is intended for traffic in a given direction.

Direction of traffic on lanes

Indicates the number of lanes and the direction of traffic on each lane. If another sign prohibiting the movement of any vehicle is displayed on sign 5.15.7, the movement of that vehicle on the corresponding lane is prohibited.

Number of lanes

Indicates the number of lanes in a given direction and the established traffic regimes on the lanes. The sign is installed in uninhabited areas at a distance of 150-300 meters from the beginning of the road section, in populated areas - at a distance of 50-150 meters.

Bus and/or trolleybus stop

Indicates a bus and/or trolleybus stop and a taxi stand.

Tram stop

Marks a tram stop and a taxi stand.

Taxi stand

Indicates a taxi stand.

Pedestrian crossing

A designated area on the road for pedestrian crossings. The sign is placed on the right side of the road. In the absence of markings on the crossing 1.14, the sign is placed at the nearest border of the crossing.

Pedestrian crossing

A designated area on the road for pedestrian crossings. The sign is placed on the left side of the road. In the absence of markings on the crossing 1.14, the sign is placed at the far edge of the crossing.

Artificial inequality

Indicates an artificial unevenness on the roadway. The sign is placed on the side of the vehicle approaching the artificial unevenness or the 1.25 marking.

Residential area

An area (zone) where the rules for traffic in a residential area apply. Signs are placed at all entrances to the residential area.

End of the residential area

Indicates the end of the residential zone marked with sign 5.21. The sign is placed at all exits from the residential zone.

Beginning of the settlement

Indicates the beginning of a settlement in which the rules for traffic in settlements apply. The sign is placed at all entrances to the settlement.

Beginning of the settlement

Indicates the beginning of a section of a developed road that does not enter any settlement and in which the rules established for traffic in settlements apply (cottage villages, separate establishments, etc.). The sign is placed at all entrances to the settlement.

End of settlement

Indicates the end of the settlement indicated by signs 5.23.1 and 5.23.2, respectively. The sign is placed at all exits of the settlement, at the actual border of the development.

End of settlement

Indicates the end of the settlement indicated by signs 5.23.1 and 5.23.2, respectively. The sign is placed at all exits of the settlement, at the actual border of the development.

Beginning of the settlement

Indicates the beginning of a settlement where the rules of traffic in settlements do not apply. The sign is placed at all entrances to the settlement.

End of settlement

Indicates the end of the settlement marked with sign 5.25. The sign is placed at all exits of the settlement, at the actual boundary of the development.

Zone with standing restrictions

Indicates an area within which parking is regulated or the maximum speed of movement is limited. The sign is placed at all entrances to the area.

End of the standing restriction zone

Indicates the end of the area marked with sign 5.27. The sign is placed at all exits.

Regulated parking zone

Indicates an area within which parking is regulated or the maximum speed of movement is limited. The sign is placed at all entrances to the area.

End of regulated parking zone

Indicates the end of the area marked with sign 5.29. The sign is placed at all exits.

Maximum speed limit zone

Indicates an area within which parking is regulated or the maximum speed of movement is limited. The sign is placed at all entrances to the area.

End of maximum speed limit zone

Indicates the end of the area marked with sign 5.31. The sign is placed at all exits.

Pedestrian zone

An area where movement is permitted only for pedestrians. Signs are posted at all entrances to the area.

End of pedestrian zone

Indicates the end of the area marked with sign 5.33. The sign is placed at all exits.

Hospital

Indicates the presence of a medical facility near the road. When passing the sign, the driver should avoid excessive noise as much as possible.

Place of merger

The point where an exit (ramp) from another road merges with a motorway.
