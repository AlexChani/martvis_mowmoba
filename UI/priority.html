Main road

A road on which a driver is also given the right of way at an unregulated intersection. The sign is placed at the beginning of the road and is repeated immediately before each intersection. 2.1 Sign 8.13 is placed in a populated area before an intersection where the main road changes direction, in an unpopulated area - 150-300 meters before the intersection and in front of the intersection.

End of the main road

The end of the road section from which it loses the status of a main road. If the main road ends before the intersection of the road on which the right of way of the given intersection is established, sign 2.2 is installed in an uninhabited area in advance together with sign 2.4, in a populated area - at a distance of 25 meters from the intersection and/or together with signs 2.3 or 2.4. The sign may also be installed at another distance indicated on the board 8.1.1.

Give way

A road on which a driver is obliged to give way to a driver moving on the road to be crossed, and in the presence of sign 8.13 – to a driver moving on the main road. Sign 2.3 is installed in an uninhabited area 150-300 meters before the intersection with sign 8.1.1, if sign 2.3 is installed before the intersection, or with sign 8.1.2, if sign 2.4 is installed before the intersection. The effect of the sign applies to the intersection of carriageways before which the appropriate sign is installed.

Non-stop movement is prohibited.

Prohibits the driver from continuing to drive without stopping in front of the "SDEK" line, and if it is not there - at the edge of the carriageway to be crossed. The driver is obliged to give way to the driver moving on the crossing road, and if there is a sign 8.13 - to the driver moving on the main road. The sign is installed if the visibility of vehicles moving on the crossing road is not ensured. It can also be installed in front of a railway crossing or quarantine checkpoint (in this case, the driver is obliged to stop in front of the "SDEK" line, and if it is not there - in front of the sign). The effect of the sign applies to the crossing of carriageways before which the appropriate sign is installed

Oncoming traffic priority

Prohibits the driver from entering a narrow section of the road if this makes it difficult to move in the opposite direction. The driver is obliged to give way to oncoming vehicles that are at the opposite entrance or on a narrow section of the road. The sign is placed directly in front of the narrow section of the road or in advance, with the sign 8.1.1.

Priority over incoming traffic

Gives the driver priority over vehicles traveling in the opposite direction when driving on a narrow section of road. The sign is placed directly in front of the narrow section of the road.
